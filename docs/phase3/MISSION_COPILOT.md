# Multi-Agent Mission Copilot Specification (Phase 3E)

**Program:** Defense Operations Intelligence Platform (DOIP)  
**Subsystem:** Multi-Agent Mission Copilot  
**Author:** MISSION_COPILOT_ENGINEER  

---

## 1. Role & Operational Invariants

The Mission Copilot acts as an interactive AI pair-planner for command staff. It does not replace human decision-making; it synthesizes multi-perspective tactical proposals.

### Invariant Guardrails
1. **Copilot Proposes, Humans Approve:** All plans, routes, asset assignments, and rules generated by the Copilot are staged in `proposed` or `draft` status.
2. **Zero Autonomous Execution:** The Copilot CANNOT transition missions to `approved` or `executing`, and CANNOT launch tasks directly.
3. **Structured Brief Format:** Every mission brief generated must contain:
   - Mission Proposal & Objectives
   - Assets Required & Readiness check
   - Fuel Requirements & PNR analysis
   - Risk Analysis & Threat Envelope assessment
   - Alternative Routes & Weather detours
   - Contingencies & Egress procedures

        ↓                       ↓                       ↓
Logistics Evaluator       Risk Assessor         Contingency Planner

                                ↓

                                ↓

---

## 3. REST / WebSocket Contract

- `POST /api/copilot/chat`: Send prompt to Mission Copilot session.
- `POST /api/copilot/propose`: Generates full structured mission package.
- `GET /api/copilot/sessions`: Lists past copilot interactive sessions.
- `POST /api/copilot/sessions/{id}/approve`: Human commander approval action.
